## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Texta is a focused notes and notebooks app for Android.

Write freely, organize pages into notebooks, and keep everything on your device. Texta does not require an account and does not sync your notes to a server.

Features
• Create and edit notes with a clean writing surface
• Organize work into notebooks
• Custom tags and formatting tools
• Haptic feedback for common actions
• Works offline — notes are stored in app-private storage on your phone

Privacy
Your notes stay on your device. Texta does not collect personal data for advertising or analytics. See the privacy policy linked on this store listing for details.

Texta is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
```
