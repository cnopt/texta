Privacy Policy

Texta (com.hanno.texta) · Last updated: 10 August 2026

Texta (“the App”) is a notes and notebooks application for Android. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

Texta stores your notes and notebooks on your device only. The App does not create user accounts, does not sync content to our servers, and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data we store on your device

When you use Texta, content you create (such as notes, notebooks, tags, and related settings) is saved in the App’s private storage on your device. This data stays under your control on that device. Uninstalling the App typically removes this local data. Android backup may include app data if device backup is enabled on your phone.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect personal information for advertising.
  • We do not operate analytics that send your notes to us.
  • We do not require sign-in or account creation.
  • We do not sell your data.

Internet access

The App may declare Internet permission for the WebView / platform runtime. Your note content is not uploaded to Texta servers as part of the core notes features described above.

Permissions

Texta may use vibration (haptics) for feedback. It does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for core note-taking features.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy if the App’s practices change (for example, if cloud sync or analytics are added later). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
